## SECTION 1: READING AND WRITING

### Q1: Choice B (augment)
- **Skill:** Words in Context
- **Why it’s correct:** The transition *"rather"* signals a contrast to *"deplete."* Furthermore, the sentence concludes with *"accelerating seedling growth."* Therefore, the ash must have increased or enhanced nutrient availability. **Augment** means to make greater, increase, or enhance.
- **Distractors:**
  - *Mitigate* means to lessen or make less severe (the opposite of what nutrients do for growth).
  - *Obscure* means to hide or make unclear.
  - *Fabricate* means to forge or invent out of thin air.

### Q2: Choice A (duplicitous)

- **Distractors:**
  - *Magnanimous* (generous/forgiving) and *exemplary* (praiseworthy) are positive traits that contradict the contrast.
  - *Fortuitous* means happening by chance or fortunate.

### Q21: Choice A ($\dfrac{7}{25}$)
- **Concept (Complementary Angle Theorem):**
  In any right triangle with acute angles $P$ and $R$:
  $$P + R = 90^\circ \implies R = 90^\circ - P$$
  The fundamental trigonometric identity states:
  $$\cos(R) = \cos(90^\circ - P) = \sin(P)$$
  Since $\sin(P) = \dfrac{7}{25}$, then $\cos(R) = \dfrac{7}{25}$.
- *Takeaway:* Whenever the SAT gives you right triangle angles $A$ and $B$, remember: **$\sin(A) = \cos(B)$ ALWAYS.**

- **Standard Geometry (Scale Factor vs. Area Factor):**
  Linear scale factor $k = \dfrac{\text{Side } DE}{\text{Side } AB} = \dfrac{24}{8} = 3$.
  When two geometric figures are similar, the ratio of their **areas** is equal to the **square** of the linear scale factor:
  $$\text{Area Ratio} = k^2 = 3^2 = 9$$
  $$\text{Area}(DEF) = 9 \times \text{Area}(ABC) = 9 \times 15 = 135\text{ cm}^2$$

### Q23: Choice B
- **Data Analysis Principle:**
  - **Mean:** When a constant $c = +15$ is added to every data point in a set, the mean increases by exactly $+15$. Therefore, $\text{Mean}(Y) > \text{Mean}(X)$.
  - **Standard Deviation:** Standard deviation measures the spread/dispersion of values relative to their mean. Adding a constant shifts the entire distribution along the number line without altering the distances between values. Therefore, **the standard deviation remains completely unchanged.**
  - Hence, Choice B is correct.

### Q25: Answer: $1$ (or $1.0$ / $1.00$)
- **Standard Algebra (Successive Percent Change):**
  - Original price: $k$
  - October reduction of $20\%$: New price $= k \times (1 - 0.20) = 0.80k$
  - November increase of $25\%$: New price $= (0.80k) \times (1 + 0.25) = 0.80k \times 1.25$
  - Calculate:
    $$0.80 \times 1.25 = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{5}{4} = 1.00$$
  - Therefore, the final price is $1.00k$, which means $m = 1$.
