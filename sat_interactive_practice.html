Official College Board SAT Math Reference Sheet

Every formula given on the real Digital SAT:

  • Circle: Area $= \pi r^2$ | Circumference $= 2\pi r$. Total degrees in circle $= 360^\circ$, Total radians $= 2\pi$.
  • Rectangle: Area $= \ell w$
  • Triangle: Area $= \frac{1}{2}bh$ | Sum of angles in a triangle $= 180^\circ$.
  • Pythagorean Theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ (Right triangles only).
  • Special Right Triangles:
    • $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$: Side ratio $= x : x\sqrt{3} : 2x$ (hypotenuse is $2x$).
    • $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$: Side ratio $= s : s : s\sqrt{2}$.
  • 3D Volume Formulas:
    • Rectangular Prism: $V = \ell w h$
    • Right Cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$
    • Sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$
    • Right Cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
    • Pyramid: $V = \frac{1}{3}\text{Base Area} \times h$
